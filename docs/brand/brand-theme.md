# Om Rudra visual identity

Theme and logo approved in this chat, 6 October 2026. Based on the current red website tokens in `frontend/website/src/index.css`. This guide supersedes the navy/orange image concept. The approved red parcel-and-route vector and white reverse variant are integrated locally. Export dimensions and usage are recorded in `exports/README.md`.

## Character

Calm, capable, approachable. Show careful handling and a clear next step. The audience is people arranging household or business moves, often on mobile. Keep the company name as editable text in layouts; do not bake it into generated photography.

## Palette

- Deep red #B51B35: logo, brand identification, small headings and links.
- Action red #D7193F: primary calls to action and selective emphasis. Do not use both reds as competing large surfaces.
- Charcoal #141416: headings, body copy, dark reverse layouts.
- White #FFFFFF: primary canvas and reverse logo.
- Light grey #F6F6F7: secondary surfaces.
- Soft rose #FFF1F3: restrained callouts.
- Muted text #5B5F6B and border #E5E5E7: supporting information.
- Green and error red remain functional UI colors, not additional brand colors.

Use white and charcoal for most of a composition, with one strong red element. Photography keeps natural colors. No navy/orange identity elements. Flat fills; no metallic, embossed, glossy or gradient logo treatments.

## Typography and shape

Sora, weight 600-700, for short headings. Inter, weight 400-500, for copy; 600 for labels. Use sentence case. Avoid all-caps paragraphs, condensed fake-premium typography, and heavy outlines. Keep text selectable on the website.

Use the site's 6 / 12 / 20 px radius scale for controls and panels; pills only for actions and compact category chips. Use generous white space and consistent left alignment. One subtle route-corner motif can connect website illustrations and social layouts. It should express movement and never sit behind important text.

## Logo brief and acceptance gates

Design the symbol first. Explore one bold, compact folded route or parcel shape with a clear forward opening. The accompanying horizontal wordmark reads "Om Rudra" with "Packers and Movers" as an optional descriptor at larger sizes. No tiny tagline inside the logo.

Do not combine house, truck, parcel, arrow and lettering into one symbol. Avoid a generic detailed truck badge. A mark must work as a single color silhouette before color is added. The route-corner symbol in the visual guide is a direction study, not the approved logo.

Required variants: deep red on white; white on red/charcoal; charcoal monochrome; horizontal symbol and wordmark; symbol-only favicon. Test favicon at actual 16, 24 and 32 px on light and dark browser-tab backgrounds. Use at least 2 px apparent features at 16 px, open negative space, and no descriptor. Do not simply shrink the horizontal logo. Test recognition beside several unrelated tab icons. Small-size tests demonstrate legibility, not trademark clearance or proven uniqueness.

Final delivery: editable SVG master, transparent PNG exports, SVG favicon plus PNG/ICO fallbacks, and clear-space/minimum-size rules. Raster generation is for exploration; final identity geometry should be cleaned and delivered as vector.

## Photography system

Shared style: realistic editorial photography, contemporary Indian settings, natural daylight, accurate skin tones, practical surroundings, consistent restrained contrast. Plain charcoal/grey uniforms, unprinted kraft boxes, unbranded vehicles. No company name, logo, watermark, headline, badges or invented signs anywhere in the image. Brand recognition comes from the page layout, typography and logo.

Process images: show one clear activity per image. Survey, protective packing, safe loading, transit, unloading, unpacking and placement. Hands, tools, wrapping and lifting must look physically plausible. Use real team photography when available. Generated scenes illustrate services; do not caption them as actual employees, fleet, facilities or completed customer moves.

Location images: one recognizable, geographically correct scene for each location. Prefer licensed real photographs for named landmarks. Check generated architecture and geography before use. No invented office/branch, city-name lettering or collage of unrelated landmarks. A state card should use a representative verified location, identified in its caption, rather than pretending to show an entire state.

Service images follow the same style, with the relevant activity or object clearly visible. Use landscape 3:2 masters for cards and 16:9 for hero/feature images. Keep the subject within the central 60% for responsive crops; reserve calm space on the intended text side of a hero. Make a deliberate portrait crop for social rather than stretching landscape files.

### Process prompt template

Realistic editorial photograph of [single moving activity] in [credible Indian setting]. [Number] movers wearing plain charcoal workwear safely handle [specific item] using [appropriate materials/tools]. Natural daylight, neutral interiors, natural skin tones, restrained contrast, believable hands and posture. Unprinted kraft boxes and completely unbranded equipment. Landscape [3:2 or 16:9], subject within central 60%, [left/right] side quiet for cropping. No text, letters, company names, logos, watermarks, labels, decorative badges or invented signage. No exaggerated luxury, unsafe lifting or artificial red color cast.

### Location prompt template

Realistic editorial travel photograph of [verified scene] in [city, state, India], showing [correct landmark or geographical feature]. Natural daylight, accurate architecture and surroundings, restrained contrast. Landscape 3:2, clear central subject, room for responsive cropping. No company names, text overlays, logos, watermarks or invented signs. Do not add moving trucks, offices or fabricated company premises. Verify against a reliable reference before publication.

## Social templates

Feed portrait: 1080 x 1350. Square alternate: 1080 x 1080. Story/reel cover: 1080 x 1920; check platform-specific overlay safe areas at export. Use a 64 px minimum outer margin on feed graphics. Keep critical story text in the central region away from interface controls.

Use three repeatable formats: moving tips (one useful point, short heading, plain illustration or relevant photograph); process explainers (one activity, photo, short caption); location guides (verified scene, editable location heading and practical route guidance). Keep one primary message and one action per asset. Place the small logo consistently at the top left; use one modest red accent or footer strip. Company name and headings may be added in the design layout, never inside the generated photo. Captions carry detailed copy.

Voice: clear, warm and practical. Use "your move", concrete preparation advice and honest scope. No fabricated reviews, awards, ratings, guaranteed prices, branch counts or government-approval claims. Avoid fear-based headlines and excessive punctuation.

## Production checklist

Confirm factual accuracy and image rights; check hands, faces and safe handling; remove accidental text/logos; test desktop/mobile crops; confirm text contrast; keep raw photos, layout files and exports separate; export responsive WebP/AVIF website variants; write descriptive alt text without promotional keyword stuffing. Keep generated-photo prompts with their assets.

## Research context

Official examples reviewed: [Allied](https://www.allied.com/), [Mayflower](https://www.mayflower.com/), and [Agarwal's Mumbai page](https://www.agarwalpackers.com/packersmoversmumbai.html). They separate services and location information, and Mayflower explicitly includes branded truck imagery. Unbranded photography is our chosen approach for flexibility, not a claim that all competitors avoid branding. Do not copy competitors' marks or photography.
