Photos tell the story. Layout carries the brand.

Process & service images

  • One believable activity in a credible Indian setting.
  • Plain charcoal workwear, unprinted kraft boxes and unbranded equipment.
  • Natural daylight and accurate skin tones.
  • Safe handling, realistic hands and practical tools.
  • No embedded names, logos, text or watermark.

Real team photos are preferred when available. Generated scenes illustrate a service.

Location images

  • One verified local scene, not a landmark collage.
  • Accurate architecture, landscape and geography.
  • Natural colors, consistent light and crop quality.
  • City names remain editable outside the photo.
  • No invented branches or company premises.
